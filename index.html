Add MediPulse to your Desktop or Mobile Homescreen for instant access & audible beep alerts.

🖥️ On Desktop (Chrome / Edge / Windows):
  1. Look at the top URL address bar of your browser.
  2. Click the Install / App icon (⊕ or computer screen icon).
  3. Click Install to add a standalone shortcut to your Windows Desktop & Taskbar!
  4. Or in Chrome/Edge menu (⋮): Select "Save and share" > "Install MediPulse".
📱 On Mobile (Android / iPhone):
  1. In Chrome on Android: Tap the ⋮ (three dots) menu and select "Add to Home screen".
  2. In Safari on iPhone: Tap the Share button (box with arrow) and tap "Add to Home Screen".
